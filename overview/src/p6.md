P6 produces configuration and procedure evidence for a Risk Management Framework package, written as code in OSCAL: inspections of the FC's parameter baseline (GNSS spoofing and jamming checks, ADS-B response, disabled uXRCE-DDS and spare MAVLink instances), of the GCS (individual logins, disk encryption, a single enabled network interface, sortie records) and of the MSS (logins, maintenance and provisioning logs, peripheral firmware versions, wired and offline key handling) (P1 `README.md`; `docs/07-requirements.md` §7.1). P6 owns {{p1.repo_reqs:P6}} requirements.

**Inherited from P2.** P2's documents at `{{p2.commit}}` hand nothing to P6 by name. *Assumption, not stated by P2: an RMF package would cite P2's evidence for SR-005, SR-010 and SR-018 once P1 records VE-07 and VE-08 as passed.*
